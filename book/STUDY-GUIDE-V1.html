DigProg Field Guide

A simple path from a 9–5 / 9–6 life to a real business — for mid-class people who will work hard after work, not for people who already have money and a network.

How to use: read one chapter → do the 24h task → watch the matching DigProg lesson → pass the quiz.

Sources: DigProg original writing. Frameworks informed by open textbooks (CC) + well-known bestsellers taught in our own words (see Best Sources map). We do not copy those books.

Who this is for

  • You have a job (or thin income) and 1–2 hours most evenings
  • You want a business or startup, not endless “side hustle tips”
  • You did not study business at university — fine
  • You will talk to real people and ask for money without fake guru tricks

Who this is not for

  • People hunting passive income with zero work
  • Scam funnels, fake scarcity, “buy my secrets”
  • Only theory with no outreach

Part 1 — Life + work foundation personality after basics

Ch 1 · Why your life feels stuck (and what changes)

  • A job pays for today; a business can buy options later
  • “Next level” is not motivation — it is a weekly system
  • Identity shift: from employee-only → builder who ships small things
  • Inspired by habit/productivity bestsellers (ideas only): tiny daily blocks beat rare heroic weeks
24h task: Block 5 evenings this week, 90 minutes each, phone in another room.

Ch 2 · Habits that make a business possible

  • Same start time after work (ritual > inspiration)
  • One outcome per session: message sent, offer written, interview done
  • Protect sleep; tired founders make expensive mistakes
  • Track streaks of actions, not hours of YouTube
  • Covey-style idea in DigProg words: put first things first = customers and offer before logos
24h task: Write your non-negotiable: “I ship ___ before I scroll.”

Ch 3 · Quitting 9–6 safely (do not jump blind)

  • Keep the job until the offer gets paid conversations repeatedly
  • Runway = months of expenses in cash
  • Risk ladder: nights/weekends → part-time leave → full leave
  • Kill criteria written before emotion hits
24h task: Write your “I quit only if ___” rule (numbers, not vibes).

Part 2 — Business & startup basics

Ch 4 · What a business is

  • Business = solve a painful problem for people who can pay
  • Startup = searching for a repeatable model under uncertainty
  • Small business = known model, executed well
  • You need: customer, problem, offer, delivery, money
24h task: One sentence: “I help ___ do ___ so they can ___.”

Ch 5 · Are you cut out for this? (interactive later in course)

  • Score 1–5: ship ugly v1 · ask for money · talk to strangers · change when wrong · 5–10h/week for 30 days
  • Low score ≠ failure — start smaller, still learn sales
  • Intrapreneur path (build inside a company) is valid
24h task: Complete the scorecard; pick one weak trait to practice.

Ch 6 · Money words (no MBA)

  • Revenue, cost, profit, cash (cash ≠ profit)
  • Price, margin, break-even
  • Ignore vanity metrics until money moves
24h task: List costs to deliver one unit of a simple digital offer.

Part 3 — Offer (Business Model thinking, DigProg-simple)

Ch 7 · Pick one idea hard enough to matter

  • Dump 20 ideas → kill unreachable / illegal / needs $10k first
  • Keep: deliver in <7 days, buyers exist in free places
  • One idea for 14 days only

Ch 8 · One-page offer + canvas thinking

  • Who / problem / promise / how it works / price / next step
  • Business Model Canvas idea (credit Strategyzer.com): value, customers, channels, revenue, costs — DigProg uses a beginner one-pager first
  • Lean idea: test assumptions fast, don’t write a 40-page plan first
24h task: Fill the one-page offer. Ugly is OK.

Ch 9 · Price before perfect

  • Underpricing burns you out
  • Ladder later: simple / medium / with help
  • Refund rule you can honor

Part 4 — Validate & find clients

Ch 10 · Talk to humans (Mom Test ideas, our words)

  • Ask what they did last time, not “would you buy?”
  • 5 real conversations > 50 polite likes
  • 48-hour validation sprint
24h task: Message 5 people with interview questions (not a pitch).

Ch 11 · First clients

  • Warm network first
  • Outbound DMs with clear ask
  • Sheet CRM: name / source / last touch / next step
  • Follow up 3×

Part 5 — Sales (biggest skill gap)

Ch 12 · Sales = helping decide

  • Discovery before pitch
  • Pain → impact → urgency → offer
  • Objections are data
  • Scripts are training wheels — use your voice

Ch 13 · Objection map

  • No money / no time / need to think / competitor / DIY
  • Practice out loud
24h task: Write 5 discovery questions + one closing line.

Part 6 — Systems, content, capstone

Ch 14 · Content that feeds sales

  • One pain question per Short
  • Batch work; cadence you can keep
  • 2 hours after work: 60 min build, 30 min outreach, 30 min content (example split)

Ch 15 · Capstone

  • Offer live · price · delivery folder · 10 outreaches sent · CRM updated · week-2 plan
  • Graduation = shipped, not “watched all videos”
