# DigProg Field Guide v1 — bullet study book

**Audience:** person with **zero** business knowledge who wants to start something real.  
**How to use:** read a chapter → do the 24h task → watch matching DigProg lessons → quiz.  
**Tone:** straight answers, no guru water.  
**Sources (rewrite + cite in final PDF):** OpenStax Entrepreneurship; Entrepreneurship Essentials; Swanson Toolkit; Innovative Business Mindset; Real Startup Book (CC). DigProg original case woven through.

---

## How this book is built
- Each chapter: **question people ask → short answer → bullets → 24h task → worksheet**
- Same spine as the online course (Modules 0–11)
- After reading end-to-end + doing tasks, you can start a first offer without another course

# Part A — Foundations

## Ch 0 — How to use DigProg
- DigProg = interactive course + this guide + quizzes that unlock the next step
- One idea per video; book holds the “why + worksheet”
- Success metric week 1: conversations started, not followers
- **24h task:** block 45 minutes tomorrow; open Module 0

## Ch 1 — What is a business?
- **Business:** create something people want and get paid for it
- Not: “content with no offer,” “ideas with no customers,” “fake urgency funnels”
- **Startup:** temporary org searching for a repeatable model under uncertainty
- **Small business:** known model, local/online execution (cafe, freelance, shop)
- DigProg teaches both: start simple, then systemize
- Building blocks: **customer → problem → offer → delivery → money**
- **24h task:** write one sentence: “I help ___ do ___ so they can ___.”

## Ch 2 — Money words (no MBA)
- **Revenue:** money in  
- **Cost:** money out to deliver  
- **Profit:** revenue − cost  
- **Cash:** what’s in the account now (you can be “profitable” and still broke)  
- **Price:** what buyer pays  
- **Margin:** (price − unit cost) / price  
- Ignore vanity: likes, empty waitlists, “impressions” with zero DMs  
- **24h task:** list every cost to deliver one unit of a simple digital product (tools, time hours × your wage)

## Ch 3 — Are you an entrepreneur?
- Entrepreneur ≠ personality cult; it’s **bias to action + learning under uncertainty**
- Traits that help: curiosity, persistence, talking to strangers, finishing small loops
- Traits that hurt if unchecked: perfectionism, endless research, fear of selling
- **Intrapreneur:** builds inside someone else’s company (valid path)
- Interactive self-check (score 1–5 each):
  - I ship ugly v1  
  - I ask for money without shame  
  - I change when evidence says so  
  - I can work 5–10h/week for 30 days  
- Low score → start with skill job + tiny side offer; still use DigProg sales module  
- **24h task:** complete scorecard; pick one trait to practice this week

## Ch 4 — When to start
- You are never “ready”; you need **minimum viable readiness**
- Need: problem you understand, way to reach 20 people, offer draft, 5–10h/week
- Don’t need: LLC day 1, logo, perfect site, ads budget
- Side path vs jump: keep survival money if risk is high
- Kill criteria: no conversations after 20 honest outreaches → change offer/audience  
- **24h task:** write start date + kill date (14 or 30 days)

# Part B — Build the offer

## Ch 5 — What you need (inventory)
- Skills you already have (language, design, coding, sales, niche knowledge)
- Time budget truthful (not fantasy 40h)
- Free tools: Docs, CapCut, Canva free, Stripe later, Notion/Sheets
- Team of 3 pattern: domain/sales, video factory, product/site
- **24h task:** asset list — skills / audience / tools / constraints

## Ch 6 — Pick one idea
- Dump 20 ideas fast  
- Kill: no urgency, no reachable buyers, illegal/ToS death, needs $10k first  
- Keep: you can deliver in <7 days, buyers hang out somewhere free  
- One idea for 14 days only  
- **One-page offer:** who / problem / promise / how it works / price / next step  
- Promise must be specific; process must be short; proof can start as “logic + case”  
- **24h task:** fill one-page offer draft

## Ch 7 — Price without panic
- Price before perfect product  
- Ladder later: Starter / Builder / Operator style  
- Beginners underprice to avoid sales — then burn out  
- Test: would you pay this for the outcome?  
- **24h task:** set a test price; write refund rule you can honor

## Ch 8 — Numbers that decide “will it work?”
- Unit cost to deliver  
- Break-even = fixed monthly costs / margin per sale (or simple: how many sales/month to cover time)  
- Time-to-first-dollar target: ≤21 days for DigProg-style digital  
- Track weekly: conversations, offers sent, closes, refunds  
- **24h task:** break-even for your offer (even if numbers are rough)

# Part C — Market & clients

## Ch 9 — Validation in 48 hours
- Talk to humans before building for months  
- 5 problem interviews > 50 survey likes  
- Ask: last time this happened? what did you try? what would you pay?  
- Polite interest ≠ buying signal  
- Smoke test: short landing + CTA + outbound  
- **24h task:** message 5 people with interview questions (not a pitch)

## Ch 10 — Where clients come from
- Warm network first (friends-of-friends, old classmates, communities you already in)  
- Outbound: DMs with value + clear ask  
- Inbound: content that answers one painful question  
- Sheet CRM: name / where found / last touch / next step / status  
- Follow-up 3×; most sales die from silence  
- **24h task:** list 20 reachable people/places; send 5 messages

# Part D — Sales (core of DigProg)

## Ch 11 — Sales is not sleaze
- Sales = help someone decide if the offer solves a real pain  
- Discovery before pitch  
- Pain → impact on life/money/time → urgency → offer  
- Objections are information  
- Scripts are training wheels; customize to your voice  
- Guarantee only what you can deliver  
- **24h task:** write your discovery 5 questions + one closing line

## Ch 12 — Objection map
- No money → smaller plan / payment timing / ROI in plain words  
- No time → smaller scope / async delivery  
- Need to think → ask what specifically; book follow-up  
- Competitor → difference in process/support, not hate  
- DIY → cost of delay  
- **24h task:** roleplay 3 objections with a friend; note weak answers

## Ch 13 — Close & deliver
- Soft close: “Want to start with Starter this week?”  
- Hard close: “I can onboard you today if you reply YES”  
- After pay: over-deliver first 48h (access, welcome, first win)  
- Ask for testimonial after first win  
- **24h task:** write welcome message template

# Part E — Distribution & systems

## Ch 14 — Content that sells
- One pain question per Short  
- Hook in 1s; payoff clear; CTA = free lesson or DM  
- Batch: 1 recording session → many cuts  
- Cadence you can keep > viral fantasy  
- DigProg ads ≠ Whop clipping accounts  
- **24h task:** write 7 hooks for your offer

## Ch 15 — Systems for a team of 3
- Scoreboard weekly: $ / conversations / lessons shipped / posts  
- Roles clear; no everyone-does-everything chaos  
- Free stack first; pay tools only after revenue  
- **24h task:** assign roles if you have partners; else calendar blocks

## Ch 16 — Capstone: ship
Checklist:
- [ ] One-page offer  
- [ ] Price + refund rule  
- [ ] Delivery folder ready  
- [ ] 10 outreach messages sent  
- [ ] CRM sheet updated  
- [ ] Week-2 plan written  
- Graduation = shipped, not “consumed all videos”
