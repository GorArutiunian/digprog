Book voice rules (non‑negotiable)

This is a real book: Amazon + PDF with every DigProg course. Author = you (professional practitioner voice).

  • Never: “mid-class,” “for people who didn’t go to university,” “simple for average people,” pity framing.
  • Never: marketing brochure tone on page 1 (“path from 9–5 to real business for tired workers”).
  • Do: first-person professional — “In my work I see…”, “Here is the rule I use…”
  • Do: respect the reader — ambitious, busy, capable.
  • Do: diagrams, tables, write-in boxes at chapter ends.
  • Audience filter is OK if factual (job / income / wants a business / not passive-income fantasy).
