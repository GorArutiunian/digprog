# L001 Storyboard — What a business is

Aligned to `voice-l001-final.mp3` / `timeline.json` (~107.9s).

## Scene 1 — Interest versus payment (0.00–10.84s)

- **Learning purpose:** Separate useful interest from paying demand.
- **Narration:** “You built an app… not of paying demand.”
- **On-screen:** App UI · reaction chips · “Paid customers: 0” · “Interest ≠ paying demand”
- **Composition:** App left, metric right; orange marker travels from praise to zero metric.
- **Entrance / exit:** App springs in; reactions stagger; metric emphasizes; soft fade to framework.
- **Motion:** Marker path; chip fades 150–250ms; no continuous camera.
- **Hold:** ~1.5s on zero metric after line lands.
- **Audio cue:** Marker arrives on “Not yet”.

## Scene 2 — The framework (10.84–23.56s)

- **Learning purpose:** Introduce Customer → Offer → Delivery.
- **Narration:** Business definition + three parts.
- **On-screen:** Definition line; three labeled nodes lighting in speech order.
- **Composition:** Centered horizontal chain on light grid.
- **Entrance / exit:** Definition first; nodes progressive; fade to customer detail.
- **Motion:** Label entrances 250–450ms; marker hops to spoken node.
- **Hold:** Brief settle after Delivery appears.

## Scene 3 — Customer (23.56–30.91s)

- **Learning purpose:** Who buys / what they need.

- **Composition:** Workspace left, questions right.
- **Motion:** Workspace fade-in; questions sequential.
- **Hold:** After second question.

## Scene 4 — Offer (30.91–37.98s)
