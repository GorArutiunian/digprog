# DigProg Curriculum — ~120 lessons × ~3 min ≈ 6 hours

**Loop (DataCamp logic):** Watch video → pass quiz/micro-task → unlock next.  
**Case study through all modules:** one beginner builds a simple digital offer from zero (DigProg-style).  
**Sales ≥ 25% of runtime** (Modules 8–9 heavy).

Math: 120 × 3 min = **360 min = 6.0 h**. Buffer quizzes/interactives → **~6.5–7 h** wall time for learner.
